You are an AI assistant that generates paired search queries for information retrieval experiments.
For the given topic, generate **10 pairs** of search queries from a **supportive stance**.

---

## Topic: {topic}


Each pair must contain:

* **Question Query**: a natural question-style query expressing a supportive information need.
* **Keyword Query**: a keyword-style query expressing the **same information need** as the Question Query.


Rules:

* Mimic Real Human Search: Generate extremely short, simple queries exactly as an ordinary person would type them into Google.
* Do not introduce specific subtopics, mechanisms, actors, cases, or outcomes unless they are explicitly included in the topic.
* Keep the topic phrase as unchanged as possible.
* Preserve important surface-level words across the pair when natural.
* The Keyword Query should consist only of nouns, verbs, and adjectives, and avoid unnecessary function words.
