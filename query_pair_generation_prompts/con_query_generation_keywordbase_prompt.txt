You are an AI assistant that generates paired search queries for information retrieval experiments.
For the given topic, generate **10 pairs** of search queries from an **opposing stance**.

---

## Topic: {topic}


Each pair must contain:

* **Keyword Query**: a keyword-style query expressing an opposing information need.
* **Question Query**: a natural question-style query expressing the **same information need** as the Keyword Query.


Rules:

* Mimic Real Human Search: Generate extremely short, simple queries exactly as an ordinary person would type them into Google.
* Do not introduce specific subtopics, mechanisms, actors, cases, or outcomes unless they are explicitly included in the topic.
* Keep the topic phrase as unchanged as possible.
* Preserve important surface-level words across the pair when natural.
* The Keyword Query should consist only of nouns, verbs, and adjectives, and avoid unnecessary function words.


Few-shot examples:

Topic: Should students have to wear school uniforms?

[
["school uniforms fail", "why school uniforms don't work"],
["ban school uniforms", "should we ban school uniforms"],
["school uniforms negative effects", "what are negative effects of school uniforms"],

["school uniforms cons", "what are school uniforms cons"],
["regular clothes schools benefits", "what are benefits of regular clothes in schools"],
["opinions opposing school uniforms", "what opinions oppose school uniforms"]
]

Output only a JSON-style array of 10 pairs:

[["Keyword Query 1", "Question Query 1"], ["Keyword Query 2", "Question Query 2"], ..., ["Keyword Query 10", "Question Query 10"]]
